What Sill does, and what it asks of you

Sill shows your Mac’s screen on your iPhone or iPad and sends what you do there back to the Mac as clicks, scrolls, gestures and keystrokes. To do that, Sill for Mac needs the Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions it asks for, and the apps need Local Network access to find each other. You choose what to stream, which devices to pair, and whether to turn on Direct Wireless Connection or Remote Access.

A paired device can see whatever you stream and control your Mac as you can, so pair only devices you trust, keep pairing codes to yourself, and remove a device in Sill’s Settings › Devices on your Mac when it should no longer connect. Use Sill only with Macs you own or are allowed to control, on networks you are allowed to use. Keep your Mac, your devices and Sill up to date.
